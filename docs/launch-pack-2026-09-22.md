## 1. Main trailer: 40 seconds

One story: discover something → save it → listen → rate → remember it.
Use separate recordings and combine them into one screen timeline in Premiere before adding the continuous phone animation in Rotato. These are editorial recommendations, not Rotato technical requirements.

| Time | Screen action | Editing purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Begin on a populated Discover screen; brief controlled scroll | Show real music immediately, without a long logo intro |
| 3–10s | Tap Save on an identifiable release, then open Listen List and hold on the same artwork | Prove where saved music goes |
| 10–17s | Mark a different release as listened, open History and hold | Show listening being logged |
| 17–27s | Rate another release, save, then hold on its visible score | Show a complete action and result |
| 27–35s | Open Profile, scroll gently through recently listened and top rated | Connect the actions to a personal music history |
| 35–40s | Settle the phone animation; show the actual RPPL logo and @rpplapp outside the phone | Give viewers one clear destination |

Capture 2–3 seconds of stillness before and after each action. Source clips should be longer than these edited segments. Keep artwork and the saved/rated outcome readable; don't speed through confirmation. Avoid dissolving between different screens inside the phone: a clean cut is clearer.

Use a different release for each demonstration, covering several genres. Keep the same demo identity throughout this trailer. Do not include Following-user demos yet. No music, captions, tap indicators or phone frames burned into source recordings; music is the owner's choice. Never show login details or real private notifications. Enable Do Not Disturb before recording and review the result.

Keep original recordings untouched. Name copies 01_Save, 02_Log, 03_Rate, 04_Profile. Match sequence dimensions and frame rate to the actual recordings; don't stretch or upscale just to hit an arbitrary size. Review the completed composite for readable text and safe margins before exporting delivery versions.

Find something you want to hear? Save it for later. When you've listened, log it and leave a rating. Add a note while it's fresh, then revisit the music that stayed with you. RPPL brings your listening list, ratings and music history together. Your taste, one release at a time.

Voiceover is optional and separate from the recordings. Leave pauses around the on-screen results. Do not imply RPPL streams music or automatically tracks listening.
